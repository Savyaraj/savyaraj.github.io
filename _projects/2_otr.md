Identified **exact time-periodic solutions** -- oscillating Turing rolls (OTRs) -- embedded within spatiotemporal chaos in the Lugiato-Lefever model, a governing equation for driven optical microresonators. This work bridges a key idea from **fluid turbulence** research -- that chaotic dynamics can be understood through unstable exact coherent states -- into **nonlinear optics**.

Discovered three families of OTR solutions bifurcating from stationary Turing roll equilibria via **Hopf bifurcations**. Demonstrated that these unstable periodic orbits act as dynamical building blocks of optical turbulence -- chaotic trajectories transiently shadow them, producing the characteristic intermittent dynamics observed in simulations and experiments.

Computed solution branches using **Newton-Krylov methods** with **Fourier spectral discretization**, characterized their stability via **Floquet analysis**, and traced their evolution through parameter space using **numerical continuation**. Mapped out a phase diagram connecting the classical regimes (continuous waves, Turing rolls, solitons, breathers) through the chaotic regime.

**Publication:** [arXiv:2509.10283](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.10283) (*Equal contribution)
