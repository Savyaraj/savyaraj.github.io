Worked on nonlinear modelling of rubber vibration isolators used in automobiles, aviation, sensitive instruments, and civil structures to protect against unwanted external vibrations. Investigated chaotic behavior in certain classes of oscillators by introducing a clearance nonlinearity element into the system model.

The project explored fractional calculus approaches for modelling viscoelastic forces, non-trivial friction and elastic force interactions, and the rich nonlinear dynamics that emerge from their combination.

**Institution:** Acoustics & Dynamics Laboratory, Ohio State University (RIYA Program, Summer 2018)
