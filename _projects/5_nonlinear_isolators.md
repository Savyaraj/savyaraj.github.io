*Acoustics & Dynamics Laboratory, Ohio State University ([RIYA Program](https://mae.osu.edu/RIYA), Summer 2018)*

Developed nonlinear models of rubber vibration isolators -- devices widely used in automobiles, aviation, sensitive instruments, and civil structures to attenuate unwanted vibrations. Focused on understanding chaotic behavior in certain classes of oscillators, a phenomenon that remains poorly characterized despite extensive prior work on isolator modelling.

Introduced a clearance nonlinearity element into the system model to capture chaotic dynamics. Built up the analysis pipeline by first simulating established models and characterizing individual nonlinearities and their interactions, then extending the framework to incorporate clearance. The work involved fractional calculus formulations for viscoelastic forces, nontrivial friction--elastic force coupling, and the rich dynamics emerging from their combination.

Part of a cohort of 6 RIYA scholars, gaining exposure to diverse mechanical engineering research. Visit the [RIYA website](https://www.mae.osu.edu/resources/previous-riya-scholars) for more information.
