Investigated how optimal amounts of noise and parameter diversity in coupled nonlinear oscillator systems can induce maximally coherent periodic behavior rather than disorder. Studied the phenomenon using coupled logistic maps -- the discrete analog of population dynamics -- focusing on the period-5 window in the bifurcation diagram.

Discovered that optimal noise in parameters pushes the coupled system toward period-5 dynamics, with the phenomenon proving robust against variations in system size and coupling strengths. Implemented a reduced mean field model for detailed analytical study and tested robustness with local (non-global) coupling configurations.

**Institution:** IIT Bombay (Nonlinear Dynamics Laboratory Course Project)
