Physicist and scientific software developer specializing in high-performance computing, numerical optimization, and nonlinear PDE simulation, with a strong track record in Python/C++ development and cross-disciplinary research.

I am a doctoral researcher at the [Emergent Complexity in Physical Systems (ECPS)](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/ecps/) laboratory at EPFL, working on numerical methods and bifurcation theory for nonlinear PDEs. My current research focuses on transferring numerical PDE-solving techniques from fluid mechanics to photonic applications for emerging frequency-comb technologies in chip-scale microresonator systems.

Previously, I worked on computational modelling of neural networks at [NeuroRestore](https://www.neurorestore.swiss/) (Lausanne University Hospital), designing data-driven simulations to analyze sensorimotor responses to epidural electrical stimulation.

I hold an MSc in Robotics with a minor in Computational Neuroscience from EPFL and a BTech in Mechanical Engineering with a minor in Physics from IIT Bombay.
